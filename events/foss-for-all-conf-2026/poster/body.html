國漢文混用體란?

What is mixed-script Korean?

國漢文混用體는 漢字語를 漢字로, 固有語와 助詞·語尾를 한글로 섞어 쓰는 表記法입니다. 20世紀 내내 新聞과 書籍, 學術 論文에 널리 쓰였습니다. 그러나 只今의 讀者 大部分은 漢字를 넉넉히 배우지 못했기 때문에, 이 時期의 記錄은 韓國語 母語 話者에게도 읽기 어렵습니다.

Korean has its own alphabet, hangul. For most of the 20th century, though, newspapers, books, and scholarship wrote Sino-Korean words in hanja (Chinese characters), keeping hangul for native words and grammatical endings. This is mixed-script Korean, 國漢文混用體 (gukhanmun honyongche). Most Korean readers today learned few hanja at school, so decades of printed records are hard to read even for native speakers.

內藏 辭典

Built-in dictionaries

  • 《標準國語大辭典》

    國立國語院이 編纂한 規範 辭典. 約 50萬 表題語.

    Standard Korean Language Dictionary: NIKL’s normative dictionary, the reference for standard spelling. About 500,000 entries.

  • 《우리말샘》

    누구나 參與하는 開放型 辭典. 100萬 餘 表題語로 現存 最大 規模.

    Open Korean Dictionary: the largest Korean dictionary, open to public contributions. Over one million entries.

  • 外來語 表記 用例

    北京 → 베이징처럼 外來語 讀音의 根據.

    NIKL loanword spelling examples: the source for readings such as 北京 → 베이징.

國立國語院이 2019年 두 辭典을 CC BY-SA 2.0 KR로 公開한 德分에 Gukhanmun이 存在할 수 있었습니다. 感謝합니다.

Gukhanmun exists because the National Institute of Korean Language (NIKL) released both dictionaries under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR in 2019. Thank you.
